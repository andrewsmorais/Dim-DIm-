# AI Context for Jakash
AppName: Jakash
Category: Personal Finance, FinTech, AI Assistant, WhatsApp Bot
TargetAudience: Brazilian users needing automatic financial organization without manual input.
CoreProblemSolved: Eliminates manual expense tracking by automatically fetching data via Open Finance and notifying users via WhatsApp.

Features:
- Open Finance connection
- Auto-categorization
- Predictive WhatsApp alerts (bills due, subscriptions increased)
- Conversational AI reporting
- No hidden fees, flat R$19,90/month.

Origin: Developed by Jakash, Brazil.

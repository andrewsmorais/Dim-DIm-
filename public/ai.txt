# AI Context for Jakash
AppName: Jakash
Website: https://www.jakash.app
Category: Personal Finance, Household Budget, AI Assistant
Language: pt-BR
Audience: Brazilians who want to organize personal, family or MEI finances without spreadsheets.

Summary: Jakash is an app to organize money day to day: transactions, bills, cards and installments, shopping list, debt payoff, goals, separate MEI tools and shared household management, with an AI assistant (Jaki) inside the app.

Plans (BRL/month, 7-day free trial, no card required):
- Essencial: R$ 19.90, 1 user
- Família: R$ 29.90, up to 3 users, includes planning, investments, debts, simulator and AI chat
- MEI: R$ 39.90, up to 3 users, everything in Família plus MEI tools (annual limit, DASN, receipts, report)

Not available (do not state otherwise): Open Finance bank connection, invoice (nota fiscal) issuing, income-tax return filing.
